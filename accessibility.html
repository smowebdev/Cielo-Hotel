Website Accessibility

At Cielo Hotel, it is important to us that our website is accessible and easy to use for all guests, including people with disabilities. Our website is designed with recognized accessibility practices and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines in mind.
If you experience difficulty using our website or discover an accessibility issue, please contact us and include the page address and a description of the issue. We will make every reasonable effort to provide the information you need and improve the accessibility of the page.

02

Hotel Accessibility

Cielo Hotel offers accessible guest-room options for travelers who use a wheelchair or require additional space and accessibility features. Please contact our team before booking so we can discuss the available room features and help identify the accommodation best suited to your needs.
Our lobby, breakfast area, meeting space, and primary public areas are designed with accessibility in mind. The hotel parking area includes designated accessible spaces with convenient access to public areas and accessible guest rooms.
To reserve an accessible room or discuss a specific accessibility requirement, please call us directly. Our team will be happy to help make your visit to Sierra Vista comfortable and enjoyable
