Why Cielo?

When we first discovered Cielo, it felt like uncovering a time capsule — a forgotten piece of Arizona rich in early mid-century character and stories waiting to be told.
We couldn't just kick sand over it and pretend we hadn't seen it. There was too much character here, too much history and too much potential.
So, we gave it a new life.
Inspired by the timeless charm of Arizona's iconic retro hotels and the unmistakable spirit of the Southwest, we've reimagined Cielo as a place where vintage style meets modern comfort. We've kept the personality, embraced the nostalgia and added everything today's traveler needs to feel right at home.

VINTAGE SOUL.
MODERN COMFORT.

Cielo isn't just about a place to sleep. It's about bringing people together.
It's for the creatively inclined, the foodies, the music buffs, the road-trippers, the adventurers and anyone who would rather surround themselves with originality than sameness.
From locally inspired art and retro details to good coffee, great music and spaces made for gathering, Cielo celebrates the things that make Southern Arizona wonderfully different.
Step outside and the story continues. The Huachuca Mountains rise in the distance, historic Bisbee is down the road, Arizona wine country is within reach, and some of the Southwest's most spectacular
landscapes are waiting to be explored.
We're proud to be part of Sierra Vista's revival — and even prouder to share this little piece of Arizona with you.

We found the treasure. We brought it back to life.
Now it's yours to discover.
Look up. Stay awhile. Welcome to Cielo.
